01

Roles and access

The application presents a different home screen depending on the signed-in account's role. Role is assigned server-side and cannot be changed from within the app.

Technician

Field staff. Sees only the work assigned to them. Can record work performed, capture evidence, and advance job status. Cannot view financial data, client records, or other technicians' assignments.

Administrator

Operations staff. Sees every project, work order, and technician submission across the organization, plus metrics, client records, proposals, and reporting.

Confirm before publishing

The backend role list is admin, moderator, user. There is no distinct technician role. Confirm which stored role the app treats as a technician, and update this section to match. If the reviewer signs in and sees an interface that contradicts this page, the rejection will repeat.

02

Technician

Sign in with the technician account. The app opens on the Jobs tab, which lists only the work assigned to the signed-in technician. Every capability below begins from that screen.

T1

Receive assigned work orders

Confirm

Reviewer asked: Receive assigned work orders.

Navigation path Sign in Jobs tab Assigned list
  1. Sign in with the technician demonstration account.
  2. The app opens on the Jobs tab (first icon in the bottom tab bar).
  3. Work orders assigned to this technician appear as cards, newest first. Each card shows the job title, client, priority, scheduled date, and current status.
  4. Use the segmented control at the top to filter by Open, In Progress, and Completed.
  5. Pull down to refresh. Newly assigned orders arrive without reinstalling the app.
  6. Tap any card to open the Job detail screen, the hub for capabilities T2 through T6.
Data source Table service_orders — fields title, description, priority, status, technician, scheduled_for, time_spent, linked to clients.
Confirm before publishing

In the backend, service_orders.technician is a free-text name, not a reference to a user account. Confirm the app can filter work orders to the signed-in technician. If it cannot, the reviewer will see either every order or none, and neither matches the claim above.

T2

Upload installation photos from the field

Confirm

Reviewer asked: Upload installation photos from the field.

Navigation path Jobs tab Job card Photos Add photo
  1. Open any job from the Jobs tab.
  2. Scroll to the Photos section of the job detail screen.
  3. Tap Add photo. Choose Take photo to use the camera on site, or Choose from library.
  4. iOS requests camera and photo library permission the first time. Both prompts explain why access is needed.
  5. Add an optional caption, then tap Upload. The photo attaches to this work order and becomes visible to administrators.
  6. Uploads captured without signal are queued and sent automatically when connectivity returns.
Confirm before publishing — highest risk item

No file storage bucket and no photo or attachment field were found in the backend. If photo upload is not present in the submitted build, do not describe it here. Either ship the feature and resubmit, or remove this claim from the manual and from the App Store description. Directing a reviewer to a screen that does not exist converts a 2.1 into a metadata rejection.

T3

Record job notes and progress updates

Confirm

Reviewer asked: Record job notes and progress updates.

Navigation path Jobs tab Job card Notes
  1. Open a job from the Jobs tab.
  2. Scroll to the Notes section.
  3. Tap Add note and type the update — parts used, conditions found, work remaining.
  4. Tap Save. The note is stamped with the author and time, and appears in reverse-chronological order.
  5. Enter hours worked in Time on job to record labor against the order.
  6. Saved notes are immediately visible to administrators reviewing the order.
Data source Table service_orders — fields description, time_spent, updated_at.
Confirm before publishing

The backend has a single description field on the work order, not a timestamped notes log. If the app overwrites one field rather than appending entries, describe it as "job notes" and remove the wording about a running history.

T4

Complete required installation checklists

Documented

Reviewer asked: Complete required installation checklists.

Navigation path Jobs tab Job card Checklist
  1. Open a job from the Jobs tab.
  2. Scroll to the Checklist section. Items appear in the order the office defined them.
  3. Tap a row to mark it complete. A gold check replaces the empty box and the progress counter advances, for example 4 of 9 complete.
  4. Tap again to clear an item if it was marked in error.
  5. A job cannot be marked Completed until every required item is checked.
Data source Table service_order_checklist — fields item, done, sort_order, keyed to service_order_id.
T5

Capture sign-offs confirming completed work

Confirm

Reviewer asked: Capture sign-offs confirming completed work.

Navigation path Job card Complete job Signature
  1. Finish the checklist, then tap Complete job at the bottom of the job detail screen.
  2. The sign-off sheet opens with a summary of work performed.
  3. Hand the device to the client. They sign with a finger in the signature panel.
  4. Enter the signer's printed name and role, then tap Save sign-off.
  5. The signature, name, and timestamp attach to the work order and the status moves to Completed.
  6. Tap Clear to redo the signature before saving.
Confirm before publishing — highest risk item

No signature, sign-off, or completion-approval field was found in the backend. Confirm this screen exists in the submitted build. If it does not, remove this capability from the manual and from the App Store description before resubmitting.

T6

Update job status in real time

Documented

Reviewer asked: Update job status in real time.

Navigation path Jobs tab Job card Status
  1. Open a job from the Jobs tab.
  2. Tap the Status control at the top of the job detail screen.
  3. Choose the new state: Open, In Progress, On Hold, or Completed.
  4. The change saves immediately and the job card in the list updates to match.
  5. Administrators see the new status on their dashboard without any further action by the technician.
  6. Adjust Priority from the same screen when a job escalates on site.
Data source Table service_orders — fields status, priority, updated_at.
03

Administrator

Sign in with the administrator account. An additional Platform tab appears in the tab bar. It is the operations suite and is the starting point for every capability below.

A1

Monitor all active projects

Documented

Reviewer asked: Monitor all active projects.

Navigation path Platform tab Project Tracker
  1. Sign in with the administrator demonstration account.
  2. Tap the Platform tab, then Project Tracker.
  3. Every project across the organization is listed with client, stage, and completion percentage.
  4. Tap a project to see its task list, with each task's completion state.
  5. Filter by stage to isolate active work from closed projects.
Data source Tables projects and project_tasks — fields name, done, sort_order.
A2

Review technician submissions, notes, and photos

Confirm

Reviewer asked: Review technician submissions, notes, and uploaded photos.

Navigation path Platform tab Service Orders Order detail
  1. Tap the Platform tab, then Service Orders.
  2. Every work order appears, regardless of which technician it belongs to.
  3. Tap an order to open it. The detail view shows the assigned technician, checklist progress, hours recorded, and current status.
  4. Scroll to Photos to view field images, and to Notes to read the technician's written updates.
  5. Scroll to Sign-off to view the captured client signature and printed name.
Confirm before publishing

This capability depends on T2, T3, and T5. Whatever is removed from those items must be removed here as well.

A3

Track operational metrics and job progress

Documented

Reviewer asked: Track operational metrics and job progress.

Navigation path Platform tab Dashboard
  1. Tap the Platform tab. The Dashboard opens by default.
  2. Summary cards show open work orders, jobs in progress, completed this period, and active projects.
  3. Profit Analysis reports margin by project, comparing quoted value against recorded labor and materials.
  4. Job Costing breaks a single project into labor and materials against budget.
  5. Tap any metric card to open the underlying records.
Data source Tables service_orders, projects, project_tasks, profit_analyses.
A4

Manage workflows and reporting

Documented

Reviewer asked: Manage workflows and reporting across the organization.

Navigation path Platform tab Clients · Products · Proposals
  1. From the Platform tab, choose a module from the menu.
  2. Client Management — client records, contacts, and site history.
  3. Product Library — the equipment catalogue with cost and sell pricing used to build quotes.
  4. Proposal Builder — assemble a quote from catalogue items and produce a client-ready document.
  5. Profit Analysis — margin reporting across projects.
  6. Create a work order from a client or project record to assign new field work.
Data source Tables clients, products, proposals, proposal_items, profit_analyses.

05

Troubleshooting

Common conditions and how to resolve them.

The Jobs list is empty

No work orders are assigned to this account, or every order is filtered out. Clear the status filter, then pull down to refresh.

The Platform tab is missing

The signed-in account is not an administrator. Sign out and sign in with an administrator account.

A photo will not upload

Confirm camera and photo permission in iOS Settings → TCL Tech Solutions. Queued uploads send automatically when signal returns.

The job will not complete

Every required checklist item must be checked, and a sign-off captured, before the status can move to Completed.

Sign-in fails

Confirm the address is verified. Use Forgot password to reset; the link opens the app directly.

Data looks stale

Pull down to refresh on any list. The app reads live data and holds no long-term local cache.
